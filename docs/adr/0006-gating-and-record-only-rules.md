# Gating and record-only Rules

Status: accepted

Every Draft is still judged before the Player sees it, but only gating Rules can reject one. A gating Rule fails at p ≥ 0.65, just above the Unsure band, and every gating failure gives the Writer a line of Retry feedback. A record-only Rule is logged and fed back at p ≥ 0.8, but never rejects a Draft. The gating Rules guard the Player and the Skeleton: Fidelity, Agency, Secret, Character drift (Core and Line), Function, World rules, Outcome, Consequence (when one is due) and Ending (on an Ending Turn). Complication and Codex are record-only. Reputation goes, and Voice and Period become direction for the Writer only.

Decided in [How a Turn resolves: Claims, consequences and Endings](https://github.com/blazephoenix/sands/issues/47), on evidence from [The Author can compare Passages with and without the Rules](https://github.com/blazephoenix/sands/issues/22): in one Turn, 10 of 13 Rule failures fell inside the Unsure band and still threw Drafts away.

## Considered Options

- **Every Rule gates, failing at p ≥ 0.5 (the old loop).** Rejected: it threw away good Drafts on answers the Judge itself rated too close to call, and pushed the Writer toward safe, flat prose.
- **Ship first, judge after, only to keep score.** Rejected: the Player could see a Passage that states a Secret or overrides their input.

## Consequences

- **Whether the Place Rule gates is open.** It waits on [What Playtest allows and what the Harvest collects](https://github.com/blazephoenix/sands/issues/49): if Canon keeps the Author's Places only, it gates in Canon and is record-only in Playtest.
- **When every Draft fails,** a Draft with no Agency or Fidelity failure ships ahead of any that has one; among the rest, the least-violating ships, as a Flagged Turn.
- **A Turn keeps 2 Drafts per Round and at most 3 Rounds,** as config to revisit with evidence.
- **The Opening is judged without the Rules that read a Player input:** Fidelity, Agency, Outcome and Consequence.
- **Prose bans return as Rules** once "Directive and prose rules" is decided.
